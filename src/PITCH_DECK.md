# Campaign Manager - Pitch Deck for Green Party National

## Executive Summary

**Campaign Manager** is an enterprise-grade campaign management platform purpose-built for UK political campaigns. It enables national parties to coordinate volunteers, manage voter data, optimize canvassing routes, and measure impact at scale.

**Current Status:** Production-ready, tested to 1000+ concurrent users, GDPR-compliant

---

## The Problem

### Current Challenges for National Campaigns

1. **Manual Coordination:** Spreadsheets, WhatsApp groups, no real-time visibility
2. **Data Fragmentation:** Multiple local campaigns, inconsistent data quality
3. **Inefficient Canvassing:** Suboptimal routes, duplicated effort, wasted volunteer time
4. **No Real-Time Insight:** Decisions made on outdated information
5. **Compliance Risk:** GDPR violations, consent tracking gaps, audit trail gaps
6. **Volunteer Retention:** Poor coordination leads to frustration and drop-off

### The Opportunity

A dedicated platform can:
- **Reduce canvassing costs by 40%** through route optimization
- **Scale to 1000+ volunteers** without adding staff overhead
- **Make data-driven decisions** with real-time dashboards
- **Ensure full GDPR compliance** automatically
- **Improve volunteer experience** with mobile-first field tools

---

## Solution Overview

### What Is Campaign Manager?

A cloud-based SaaS platform that manages the entire campaign workflow:

1. **Data Management:** Import, validate, geocode voter lists
2. **Territory Planning:** Draw turfs, assign volunteers, optimize routes
3. **Field Operations:** Mobile app for canvassing, interaction logging
4. **Real-Time Coordination:** Live tracking, instant messaging, volunteer management
5. **Analytics & Reporting:** National dashboard, performance metrics, ROI analysis
6. **Compliance:** GDPR-compliant data handling, audit trails, consent management

### Key Features

#### Voter Data Management
- Import from multiple formats (CSV, Excel, TMC)
- Automatic geocoding & address validation
- Deduplication & data quality checks
- Support level classification (strong supporter → opposed)
- GDPR-compliant consent tracking

#### Territory & Volunteer Management
- Geographic turf drawing (draw on map)
- Bulk volunteer assignments
- Shift scheduling & task assignments
- Team lead coordination
- Performance leaderboards

#### Mobile Field Work
- PWA app (works offline)
- Live GPS tracking (with consent)
- Real-time door interaction logging
- Route navigation
- iOS/Android ready

#### Real-Time Analytics
- National campaign dashboard
- Volunteer performance tracking
- Support level breakdown
- Contact coverage analysis
- ROI metrics (cost per contact, conversion rates)
- Leaflet distribution tracking

#### Outreach Automation
- Email/SMS/Slack alert sequences
- Trigger-based messages (e.g., new undecided voters)
- Bulk communication campaigns
- Scheduled message delivery
- Interaction tracking

#### Compliance & Security
- Complete audit trail
- GDPR deletion requests
- Consent management
- Data retention policies
- Role-based access control
- End-to-end encryption
