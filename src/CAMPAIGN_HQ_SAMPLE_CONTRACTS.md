## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **"Software"** means the Campaign HQ web-based platform, including all features, tools, dashboards, APIs, and associated documentation.

1.2 **"Subscription Plan"** means the pricing tier selected by the Licensee (Starter, Professional, or Enterprise) as detailed in Schedule 1.

1.3 **"Campaign"** means a discrete electoral campaign instance created within the platform.

1.4 **"Users"** means campaign organisers, volunteers, and staff authorised by the Licensee to access the Software.

1.5 **"Personal Data"** has the meaning given in the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018.

---

## 2. GRANT OF LICENCE

2.1 Campaign HQ grants the Licensee a **non-exclusive, non-transferable, revocable licence** to access and use the Software during the Subscription Term, subject to the terms of this Agreement.

2.2 The licence is limited to the number of Campaigns, contacts, and Users specified in the Licensee's Subscription Plan (Schedule 1).

2.3 The Licensee may not sub-licence, resell, or otherwise transfer access to the Software to any third party without prior written consent from Campaign HQ.

---

## 3. SUBSCRIPTION TERM & FEES

3.1 The Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues on a **rolling monthly basis** unless either party gives 30 days' written notice of termination.

3.2 Fees are as set out in Schedule 1. Payment is due monthly in advance by direct debit or card.

3.3 Campaign HQ reserves the right to increase fees on 60 days' written notice. The Licensee may terminate within that notice period without penalty if they do not accept the new fees.

3.4 All fees are exclusive of VAT, which will be added where applicable.

3.5 A **30-day free trial** is offered to new Licensees. No payment is taken during the trial period. The Licensee may cancel before the trial ends with no obligation.

---

## 4. ACCEPTABLE USE

4.1 The Licensee agrees to use the Software solely for lawful political campaigning purposes within the United Kingdom.

4.2 The Licensee must not use the Software to:
- Process data of individuals without appropriate legal basis or consent;
- Engage in unlawful harassment, discrimination, or targeted disinformation;
- Violate Electoral Commission rules or applicable UK electoral law;
- Introduce malicious code, attempt to reverse-engineer, or circumvent access controls.

4.3 The Licensee is responsible for ensuring all Users comply with this Agreement and applicable law.

---

## 5. DATA PROTECTION

5.1 Each party agrees to comply with the UK GDPR, the Data Protection Act 2018, and any applicable guidance from the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

5.2 The Licensor acts as a **Data Processor** in relation to any Personal Data processed on behalf of the Licensee. The Licensee acts as the **Data Controller**.

5.3 Campaign HQ will:
- Process Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Licensee;
- Implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures;
- Assist the Licensee in responding to data subject rights requests;
- Not transfer Personal Data outside the UK/EEA without appropriate safeguards;
- Delete or return all Personal Data upon termination of this Agreement.

5.4 A full Data Processing Agreement (DPA) is available upon request and forms part of this Agreement when signed.

---

## 6. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

6.1 All intellectual property rights in the Software, including code, design, algorithms, and documentation, remain the sole property of William Mark Bradley / Campaign HQ.

6.2 The Licensee retains all ownership of data inputted into the Software ("Client Data"). Campaign HQ claims no rights over Client Data.

6.3 The Licensee grants Campaign HQ a limited licence to process Client Data solely to provide the Software services.

---

## 7. CONFIDENTIALITY

7.1 Each party agrees to keep confidential all non-public information received from the other party and not to disclose it to any third party without prior written consent.

7.2 This obligation does not apply to information that is publicly known, already known to the receiving party, or required to be disclosed by law.

---

## 8. WARRANTIES & LIABILITY

8.1 Campaign HQ warrants that it will provide the Software with reasonable skill and care, and that the Software will substantially conform to its published documentation.

8.2 Campaign HQ does not warrant that the Software will be uninterrupted or error-free. Planned maintenance will be communicated in advance.

8.3 **Limitation of Liability:** To the maximum extent permitted by law, Campaign HQ's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid by the Licensee in the 12 months preceding the claim.

8.4 Neither party shall be liable for indirect, consequential, or special losses, including loss of profits or electoral outcome.

---

## 9. TERMINATION

9.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement on 30 days' written notice.

9.2 Campaign HQ may terminate immediately if the Licensee materially breaches this Agreement and fails to remedy the breach within 14 days of written notice.

9.3 On termination, the Licensee's access to the Software will cease and Campaign HQ will make Client Data available for export for 30 days, after which it will be securely deleted.

---

## 10. GOVERNING LAW

10.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales. Any disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.

## RECITALS

A. Campaign HQ has developed a proprietary political campaign management platform designed for UK electoral operations.

B. The Green Party wishes to obtain an exclusive national licence to use the platform across all its constituency and ward campaigns in England and Wales.

C. The parties wish to formalise the terms of that exclusive arrangement in this Agreement.

---

## 1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 **"Platform"** means the Campaign HQ software platform in its entirety, including all features, updates, APIs, and associated services.

1.2 **"Exclusive Territory"** means England and Wales (or as otherwise agreed in Schedule 2).

1.3 **"Licence Term"** means the initial fixed term as set out in Schedule 2, subject to renewal.

1.4 **"Authorised Campaigns"** means all Green Party constituency, ward, regional, and national electoral campaigns operating within the Exclusive Territory.

1.5 **"White-Label"** means the ability to brand the Platform with the Green Party's own visual identity (logo, colours, domain).

---

## 2. GRANT OF EXCLUSIVE LICENCE

2.1 Campaign HQ grants the Green Party an **exclusive, non-transferable licence** to use the Platform across all Authorised Campaigns within the Exclusive Territory during the Licence Term.

2.2 **Exclusivity** means that Campaign HQ will not licence the Platform to any other political party operating within the Exclusive Territory during the Licence Term.

2.3 This exclusivity is conditional upon the Green Party:
- Maintaining timely payment of the Licence Fee;
- Achieving minimum usage thresholds as set out in Schedule 2 (where applicable);
- Not being in material breach of this Agreement.

2.4 The Green Party may not sub-licence or resell access to the Platform to third parties, including affiliated parties or independent candidates, without prior written consent.

---

## 3. LICENCE TERM & FEES

3.1 The initial Licence Term shall be **[2 / 3 / 5 years]** commencing on the Effective Date, as agreed in Schedule 2.

3.2 The annual Licence Fee is set out in Schedule 2. Payment is due annually in advance, or quarterly as agreed.

3.3 The Licence Fee is fixed for the initial Licence Term, providing pricing certainty for budgeting and election cycle planning.

3.4 At the end of the initial Licence Term, the Agreement will renew for successive 12-month periods at a rate to be mutually agreed, with a minimum of 6 months' notice of any fee change.

3.5 All fees are exclusive of VAT.

---

## 4. WHITE-LABEL & BRANDING

4.1 Campaign HQ will provide a white-label configuration of the Platform using the Green Party's brand identity, including:
- Custom domain (e.g., campaigns.greenparty.org.uk);
- Green Party logo and colour scheme throughout the interface;
- Removal of "Campaign HQ" branding from all user-facing interfaces;
- Custom email domain for system notifications.

4.2 Campaign HQ retains all underlying intellectual property rights in the Platform. White-labelling does not transfer any IP to the Licensee.

4.3 The Green Party grants Campaign HQ a limited licence to use its logo and brand assets solely for the purpose of implementing the white-label configuration.

---

## 5. IMPLEMENTATION & ONBOARDING

5.1 Campaign HQ will provide a dedicated onboarding programme including:
- Platform configuration and white-label setup (target: within 4 weeks of signing);
- Staff and volunteer training sessions (up to [X] sessions included);
- Data migration support for existing contact databases;
- A dedicated account manager for the duration of the Licence Term.

5.2 Implementation milestones and timelines are set out in Schedule 3.

---

## 6. SERVICE LEVELS (SLA)

6.1 Campaign HQ commits to the following service levels during the Licence Term:

| Metric | Commitment |
|--------|-----------|
| Platform Uptime | 99.5% per calendar month (excl. planned maintenance) |
| Planned Maintenance | Outside of core campaign hours; 48 hours' notice |
| Critical Bug Response | Within 4 hours (acknowledgement), 24 hours (resolution target) |
| Non-critical Bug Response | Within 2 business days |
| Support Hours | Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm UK time (emergency line during elections) |

6.2 Service credits will apply if uptime falls below the committed level, as set out in Schedule 2.

---

## 7. DATA PROTECTION

7.1 The parties will enter into a full Data Processing Agreement (DPA) as a condition of this Agreement.

7.2 All voter and contact data processed through the Platform remains the sole property of the Green Party.

7.3 Campaign HQ will implement ISO 27001-aligned security controls and provide annual security attestation upon request.

7.4 Campaign HQ will notify the Green Party of any personal data breach within 24 hours of becoming aware, to enable the Green Party to meet its ICO notification obligations.

7.5 Upon termination, Campaign HQ will provide a full data export and securely delete all Green Party data within 30 days.

---

## 8. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

8.1 All rights in the Platform, including future enhancements and updates, remain with William Mark Bradley / Campaign HQ.

8.2 The Green Party may request custom feature development. Any bespoke features built exclusively for the Green Party will be jointly agreed in writing, with IP ownership negotiated on a case-by-case basis.

8.3 Generic platform improvements inspired by feedback from the Green Party will remain the property of Campaign HQ.

---

## 9. EXCLUSIVITY PROTECTION & NON-COMPETE

9.1 During the Licence Term, Campaign HQ will not:
- Licence the Platform to any other political party operating within the Exclusive Territory;
- Provide materially equivalent services to a direct political competitor of the Green Party within the Exclusive Territory.

9.2 For the avoidance of doubt, Campaign HQ may continue to serve campaigns outside the Exclusive Territory and non-political clients at any time.

---

## 10. TERMINATION

10.1 Either party may terminate this Agreement by giving **6 months' written notice** prior to the end of the initial Licence Term or any renewal term.

10.2 Campaign HQ may terminate immediately on written notice if:
- The Licensee fails to pay any Licence Fee within 30 days of the due date;
- The Licensee materially breaches this Agreement and fails to remedy within 28 days of notice.

10.3 The Green Party may terminate immediately if Campaign HQ commits a material breach relating to data security or exclusivity obligations.

10.4 On termination, Campaign HQ will provide a 90-day wind-down period during which the Platform remains accessible to enable data export and transition.

---

## 11. WARRANTIES & LIABILITY

11.1 Campaign HQ warrants that:
- It has the right to grant this licence;
- The Platform does not infringe any third-party intellectual property rights;
- It will maintain appropriate insurance throughout the Licence Term.

11.2 **Limitation of Liability:** Campaign HQ's aggregate liability shall not exceed 12 months' Licence Fees paid. Neither party shall be liable for indirect losses or loss of electoral outcome.

---

## 12. GOVERNING LAW & DISPUTE RESOLUTION

12.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.

12.2 The parties agree to attempt to resolve disputes through good-faith negotiation before resorting to formal proceedings.

12.3 Any unresolved disputes shall be referred to mediation before litigation, with costs shared equally unless otherwise ordered.
