## Executive Summary

Base44 is a comprehensive campaign management platform used by 50+ political campaigns to coordinate volunteer canvassing, manage voter data, optimize routes, track territory coverage, and win elections.

**Key Stats:**
- **500K+ voter contacts** managed across campaigns
- **1000+ volunteers** coordinated in real-time
- **50+ active campaigns** (local, regional, national)
- **SOC 2 certified**, GDPR compliant
- **99.9% uptime** with dedicated support

## Core Platform Modules

### 1. **Voter Contact Management**
- Import from electoral registers (CSV, Excel, TMC, JSON, PDF)
- Automatic postcode extraction and geocoding
- Contact deduplication and validation
- Full contact history and interaction tracking
- Support level scoring (Strong/Leaning/Undecided/Opposed)
- Bulk tagging and segmentation
- GDPR consent management and audit trail

**Key Pages:** Contacts, Data Import, Voter List Import, Contact History

---

### 2. **Mobile Field Canvassing**
- **iOS & Android native apps** (PWA, offline-capable)
- Location-based contact sorting (proximity detection)
- Real-time door knock logging with outcomes
- Interactive scoring system for voter sentiment
- Offline-first sync (no data loss even without connection)
- Automatic welfare check-in alerts (30-minute intervals)
- Live leaderboards with performance tracking
- Session summary and quick reporting

**Key Pages:** Field Mode, Canvassing Dashboard, Leaderboard

---

### 3. **Territory & Turf Management**
- Interactive map-based turf drawing and creation
- Automatic boundary detection from legacy DOCX maps
- Volunteer assignment to turfs with door targets
- Three-round leaflet distribution tracking (postal, non-postal, all)
- Turf status tracking (unassigned, assigned, in-progress, completed)
- Street-level leaflet run sheets with safety briefing
- Turf density mapping and coverage visualization

**Key Pages:** Turf Management, Leaflet Tracker, Leaflet Print Sheets, TurfDensity Map

---

### 4. **Route Optimization & Navigation**
- AI-powered nearest-neighbor route planning
- Postcode-based clustering and sorting
- House number sequencing for natural walking order
- Interactive route visualization on map
- Turn-by-turn field navigation
- PDF and printable walk sheets with contact details
- Route performance tracking and analytics

**Key Pages:** Route Optimizer, Field Navigation

### 5. **Volunteer Coordination**

- Volunteer profile setup with availability and preferences
- Automatic team assignment based on skills/location
- Real-time volunteer location tracking (with consent)

- GDPR-compliant volunteer registration

**Key Pages:** Shift Management, Volunteer Assignments, Volunteer Profiles, Volunteer Calendar

- **Hosted on:** AWS (GovCloud-ready)

## Key Differentiators

### 1. **Offline-First Mobile App**
Volunteers can door-knock in areas with no signal. All data syncs automatically when reconnected.

### 2. **AI-Powered Route Optimization**
Intelligent nearest-neighbor algorithms reduce volunteer walk time by 20-30%, enabling more doors per shift.

### 3. **Real-Time Welfare Tracking**
Automatic 30-minute check-ins with GPS tracking and emergency alerts keep volunteers safe.

### 4. **Automatic Turf Boundary Detection**
Import legacy DOCX maps and auto-extract boundaries and street lists — no manual re-mapping.

### 5. **GDPR-by-Default**
Built-in right-to-be-forgotten automation, consent tracking, audit logs, and data retention controls.

### 6. **Electoral Register Integration**
Directly import electoral spreadsheets with automatic postcode extraction and turf zone tagging.

### 7. **National Multi-Campaign Coordination**
Single dashboard for managing unlimited campaigns, candidates, and constituencies — perfect for party infrastructure.
