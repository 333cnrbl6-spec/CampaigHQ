# Green Party National Campaign Platform
## Executive Pitch Deck

---

## SLIDE 1: THE PROBLEM

**Current state (spreadsheets + volunteers + chaos):**
- 650 local campaigns managing voter contacts independently
- No coordination between wards/constituencies
- Data scattered across Excel, paper, various systems
- 10,000+ volunteers with no unified workflow
- Real-time visibility: impossible
- Compliance tracking: manual, error-prone
- Election cycles: high-stress rebuilds each time

**Cost of status quo:**
- 2 FTE staff managing data flows = £80K/year
- Risk of GDPR violations = legal exposure
- Missed volunteer insights = lower conversion rates
- No post-election analysis = no learning

---

## SLIDE 2: THE SOLUTION

**One platform. All campaigns. Real-time coordination.**

Campaign Manager is a fully-built, production-ready platform for UK electoral campaigns:
- **Volunteer coordination** — Assign 10K+ volunteers to turfs, track real-time progress
- **Contact management** — Import, deduplicate, geocode, and track 10M+ voter contacts
- **Field canvassing** — Mobile app for door-to-door with offline sync, welfare checks, leaderboards
- **National dashboard** — See all 650 campaigns + aggregate metrics in one view
- **Compliance automation** — GDPR consent tracking, right-to-be-forgotten, audit logs
- **Reporting & analytics** — Turnout, support levels, volunteer performance by region

**Already built, tested, and battle-hardened.**
