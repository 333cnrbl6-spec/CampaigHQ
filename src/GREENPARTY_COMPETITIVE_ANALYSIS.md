## 1. WHAT GREEN PARTY CURRENTLY USES

### Primary Tools (Confirmed)
1. **Ecanvasser** (formerly known as eCanvasser)
   - Current tool for voter outreach & issue tracking
   - Canvassing-focused platform
   - Limitations: Manual, local-campaign-specific, limited integration
   - No unified cross-party coordination

2. **Action Network**
   - Digital platform for supporter management
   - Event organization
   - Campaign coordination
   - Limitations: Supporter/activist focused, not voter-centric

3. **NationBuilder** (Some Local Campaigns)
   - Some regional Green parties use this
   - CRM + voter management
   - Used by international Green parties (Belgium, Netherlands)
   - Limitations: Expensive for volunteer-run organizations, US-centric

### Recent Infrastructure Moves
- **Actively Hiring**: Database Administrator & Digital Officers (Voter Engagement)
  - Clear signal: Party recognizing digital infrastructure gap
  - Restricted funding allocated for "Green Party Digital Team" development
  - This is YOUR opening — they're ready to invest in solutions

### Integration Gaps
- **No unified national platform** connecting 200+ local parties
- **No cross-campaign volunteer coordination** system
- **No voter targeting/segmentation** at scale
- **Limited automation** for outreach sequences
- **No multi-channel** (email/SMS/post) coordination
- **Decentralized data** — each local party has own systems

## 8. RED FLAGS TO ADDRESS IN PITCH

### Q: "Why can't we just use NationBuilder?"
**A**: NationBuilder is £500+/month per party, designed for donors not volunteers, and 200 campaigns would cost £120k/month. We're purpose-built for your multi-local-party model at 1/4 the cost.

### Q: "Why not expand Ecanvasser?"
**A**: Ecanvasser is single-campaign. We're tackling multi-party coordination, which is a fundamentally different problem. We're a national layer on top of local canvassing.

### Q: "What about data security?"
**A**: We're pursuing SOC 2 compliance, end-to-end encryption, role-based access control, and full audit trails. GDPR is built-in from day one.

### Q: "How do we migrate from Ecanvasser?"
**A**: We handle the data migration. No cost, no downtime. And Ecanvasser can continue running in parallel during beta.

### Q: "What's the timeline?"
**A**: Core platform now (you've seen it). GDPR + electoral data by end of Q2. Beta with 10 parties Q3. National rollout for 2029 election campaign.
