## 4. UK ELECTORAL GEOGRAPHY: WARDS, CONSTITUENCIES & POSTCODES

### Electoral Hierarchy
```
Country (England & Wales)
  ├─ Region (e.g., South West, London)
  │   ├─ County/Council Area
  │   │   ├─ District/Local Authority (Metropolitan/Non-Metropolitan)
  │   │   │   ├─ Ward (local council election unit)
  │   │   │   │   └─ Polling Districts (smaller voting units)
  │   │   │   └─ Postcode Sectors (CH65, SK22, etc.)
  │   │   └─ Postcode Districts (CH, SK, etc.)
  │   └─ Parliamentary Constituency
  │       └─ Multiple Wards per Constituency
  └─ Scotland/NI (devolved)
```

### Ward Structure
- **Local Council Elections** fought ward-by-ward (typically 2-3 councillors per ward)
- **~7,000 wards** in England & Wales total
- Wards are subdivisions within local authority areas (councils)
- Green Party target wards based on:
  - Previous vote share
  - Demographic/voter analysis
  - Volunteer availability
  - Party resource allocation

### Constituency Structure
- **650 constituencies** in UK Parliament
- Each constituency = multiple wards (usually 8-12)
- General elections fought seat-by-seat
- Green focus: build local strength first, then aim for parliamentary seats

### Postcode Data
- **Royal Mail Postcode System** — 1.8+ million postcodes in UK
- Structure: `A(A)NN(A/N)NN` (e.g., `SW1A 1AA`)
- **Postcode Districts** (e.g., SW1, E1) — roughly 120 districts cover England & Wales
- **Postcode Sectors** (e.g., SW1A) — ~2,000 sectors, finer granularity for micro-targeting
- **Official Data Source**: UK Office for National Statistics (ONS) Open Geography Portal
- **Boundary Data**: Boundary Commission for England, Electoral Calculus, Ordnance Survey

### Sub-Ward Organization (Turf Mapping)
- For canvassing efficiency: streets/postcodes further subdivided into **"turfs"** (2-3 hour canvassing blocks)
- **Turf = ~50-100 properties** grouped by geography
- **Turf Assignment** = core volunteer coordination tool
- **Turf Sheets** = canvasser route cards with contact lists

## 5. CAMPAIGN MANAGEMENT BEST PRACTICES (Industry Standard)

### Voter Contact Strategy
1. **Initial Contact** — leafleting, canvassing, phone banking
2. **Supporter Identification** — gauge support level (strong/lean/undecided/opposed)
3. **Persuasion** — targeted messaging for undecideds
4. **Turnout** — GOTV (Get Out The Vote) reminder campaigns day-before & election day

### Volunteer Workflow
- **Recruitment** → **Training** → **Assignment** → **Execution** → **Feedback**
- **Shift Management**: schedule volunteers for 2-3 hour blocks
- **Task Variety**: canvassing, leafleting, phone banking, event coordination, admin
- **Retention**: badge system, public recognition, tangible impact metrics

### Data Infrastructure
- **Voter File/Database**: contact records + interaction history
- **Canvassing Logs**: doors knocked, support levels captured
- **Volunteer Tracking**: hours, turfs completed, skill development
- **Real-time Dashboards**: live campaign progress, volunteer leaderboards
- **Reporting**: weekly/monthly impact summaries for team morale

### Comparable Tools (Industry)
- **NationBuilder** — used by international green parties (Groen in Belgium/Netherlands)
- **CanvassingHub** — professional political canvassing platform
- **Solidarity Tech** — progressive organizing + volunteer coordination
- **VolunteerHub** — volunteer management for campaigns
- **Trail Blazer** — CRM for campaigns/PACs with voter, donor, volunteer tracking
